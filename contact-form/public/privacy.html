Privacy

How I look after your details

I am Elin Marsh, and I run Fernlea Counselling. I am responsible for the details you send me. If you have a question about them, email hello@example.co.uk.

What I collect

When you use the contact form, I keep what you type: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, the topic you choose and your message. I also keep the date and time you sent it.

Why I keep it

  • To reply to you and arrange a first call.
  • Because you have asked me to, by sending the form.

What you tell me about your health or feelings is extra sensitive. I use it only to understand how I can help.

Who can see it

Only me. Your message is stored by Cloudflare, which runs this website, behind a password. I never sell or share your details, and I do not use them for marketing.

How long I keep it

If we don’t work together, I delete your message after 12 months. If we do, I keep notes as my professional body asks, and I tell you how long in our first session.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time. Email me and I will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how I handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. The spam check on the form, Cloudflare Turnstile, may set a cookie that it needs to work.

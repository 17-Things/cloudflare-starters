    Questions

    Things people often ask

    If your question isn’t here, ask it in your message. There are no silly questions.

    Is what I say kept private?

    Yes. What you say stays between us. There are rare limits, for example if I think someone is at serious risk of harm. I explain these in our first call, so there are no surprises.

    How many sessions will I need?

    It depends on you. Some people feel better after six sessions. Others like to keep going for longer. We check in together every few weeks, and you can stop whenever you want.

    What happens in the free call?

    We talk for about 20 minutes. You tell me a little about what is going on, and you can ask me anything. At the end, there is no pressure to book. Many people take a few days to decide.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Please give me 48 hours’ notice and there is no charge. Life happens, so I am flexible when something comes up at short notice.

    Do you see young people or couples?

    I work with adults over 18, one to one. If you need something else, I am happy to suggest someone I trust.

    Can I pay with my health insurance?

    Some insurers cover counselling. Ask your insurer first, then tell me which company it is in your message, and I will check.

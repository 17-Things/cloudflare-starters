Our story

Two bakers, one old oven and a lot of flour.

We opened Wren & Rye in 2014 in the old print shop on Mill Lane. The oven came from a bakery that was closing down. It still does most of the work.

We bake the way we like to eat: slowly, with good flour, real butter and not much else. Our sourdough takes two days. Our croissants take three.

Most of our team live within a mile of the shop. Many of our customers do too. We know a lot of you by your usual order, and we like it that way.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Do I need to book a table?

No. All our tables are walk-in. For a group of six or more, call us the day before and we will do our best.

Can I order a cake or bread to collect?

Yes. Give us 48 hours' notice by phone or email. Celebration cakes start at £32 and feed about twelve.

Do you cater for allergies and vegan diets?

Every item on the counter has a label with its allergens. We always have something vegan. We use nuts, gluten and dairy in one small kitchen, so we cannot promise that anything is free from traces.

Is there step-free access and parking?

Yes. The front door is level with the street and we have an accessible toilet. The nearest free car park is on the High Street, two minutes' walk away. There is a cycle rack outside.

Are dogs welcome?

Always. There is a water bowl by the door and a jar of dog biscuits on the counter.
